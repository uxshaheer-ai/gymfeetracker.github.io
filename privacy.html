Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Gym Fees Android app ("the app", "we") uses and how. In short: your data stays on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We do not run any server and we never receive your data.

1. Information the app stores

You enter information about your gym members into the app, such as names, phone numbers, optional photos, fee amounts and payment dates. This information is stored only on your device.

2. Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup

Signing in with Google is optional and is only used for backup. If you sign in, the app asks Google for:

  • Your name and email address (profile, email) — used only to show which Google account the backup belongs to. It is saved on your device.
  • Access to the app's own hidden folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata) — used only to save and restore your Gym Fees backup file. The app cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and the folder is not visible in your Drive.

Your backup travels directly between your phone and Google. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Gym Fees' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Camera and photos

If you add a member photo, the app uses your camera or photo library only to pick that picture. The photo is stored with your data on your device (and in your Drive backup, if turned on).

4. WhatsApp reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app opens WhatsApp with a reminder message and the member's number filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages you send there.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not use your data to train any AI or machine-learning model.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings. To delete your Drive backup, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Gym Fees and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children

The app is meant for gym owners and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

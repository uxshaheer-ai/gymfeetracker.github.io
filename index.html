Gym Fees

A simple Android app that helps gym owners keep track of their members and who has paid this month's fee.

What it does

  • Keep a list of your gym members, with their phone number and an optional photo.
  • Mark monthly fees as paid and see at a glance who still owes.
  • Optionally open WhatsApp with a ready-made fee reminder for a member. You press Send yourself.
  • Optionally back up your data to your own Google Drive, so you can restore it on a new phone.

Your data stays yours

Gym Fees has no servers of its own. Your member list is stored on your phone. If you turn on Google Drive backup, the backup goes to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive that only Gym Fees can see. We never receive or see your data.
